 Project 5: Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

 Overview

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore retail sales dataset using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA). The objective is to understand sales and profit trends across different regions, product categories, and customer segments. Data cleaning, statistical analysis, grouping, and visualization techniques are used to generate meaningful business insights.

---

 Objective

- Import and clean the dataset.
- Handle missing values and duplicate records.
- Perform filtering and sorting.
- Calculate sales and profit statistics.
- Analyze region-wise and category-wise performance.
- Create data visualizations.
- Generate business insights and recommendations.

---

 Dataset

- **Dataset Name:** Sample-Superstore.csv
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Description:** The dataset contains retail order information, including customer details, product categories, sales, profit, discount, quantity, shipping details, and regional information.

 Executive Summary

The Sample Superstore dataset was analyzed using Python-based data analytics techniques. The dataset was cleaned by removing duplicate records and checking for missing values. Statistical analysis and GroupBy operations were performed to understand sales and profit trends across different regions and product categories.

Visualizations helped identify sales patterns, profit distribution, and the relationship between sales and profit. These insights support better business decision-making and highlight opportunities for improving overall performance.
